Step-by-Step Guide

How the Scholarship Portal Works

A fast, 4-step workflow from application submission to grant disbursement.

1

Choose Scheme

Select the scholarship matching your academic level, GPA, and family income limit.

2

Apply Online

Fill out personal, institutional, and bank details and upload your marksheets.

3

Officer Verification

Authorized evaluators verify marksheets, college bonafides, and income certificates.

4

Fund Disbursement

Approved funds are disbursed directly to your registered bank account via DBT.
